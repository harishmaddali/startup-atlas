# Startup Atlas

Startup Atlas is a source-backed directory of startups and the capital and support ecosystem available to their founders.

## Language

**Startup**:
A company building a scalable product or service and represented by an existing company profile.
_Avoid_: Ecosystem entity, organization

**Organization**:
A founder-recognizable firm, network, institution, or agency that provides startup capital or support. Legal fund vehicles managed by the same firm belong to the organization rather than becoming separate organizations.
_Avoid_: Fund, provider, company

**Angel**:
An individual with publicly verifiable personal or syndicate-led startup investment activity during the preceding 24 months.
_Avoid_: Mentor, investor profile

**Program**:
A specific, actionable opportunity offered by an organization, such as an incubation intake, accelerator cohort, grant, challenge, fellowship, or government scheme.
_Avoid_: Organization, event

**Location**:
A publicly documented office, campus, or incubation facility associated with an organization, angel, or program. A service area is not a location.
_Avoid_: Coverage, geography

**India-wide**:
An organization, angel, or program that demonstrably serves Indian startups but has no public India location appropriate for a map pin.
_Avoid_: Remote location, India pin

**Live Program**:
A program accepting applications, explicitly accepting rolling interest, or scheduled to open within 90 days.
_Avoid_: Active organization, historical program

**Evidence**:
A regulatory, government, official, or reputable published source that supports one or more profile claims.
_Avoid_: Reference, confidence

**Location Precision**:
The geographic specificity of a map point: building, street, neighborhood, city, or none. It does not describe the quality of the profile's other evidence.
_Avoid_: Data confidence, verification status

**Coverage Area**:
A city, metro cluster, or region whose ecosystem sources and unresolved leads are tracked as one research unit.
_Avoid_: Location, service area
